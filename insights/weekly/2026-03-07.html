Weekly Market Recap March 07, 2026

Markets Drop Amid Escalating Iran Conflict

Key Takeaways

  • The S&P 500 fell by 2.0% and the Dow Jones declined by 2.9% in the week ending March 7, 2026, due to escalating conflict in Iran and subsequent supply chain disruptions.
  • Sectors closely tied to global trade, such as Materials and Industrials, experienced significant declines of 6.4% and 5.0%, respectively, while the Energy sector was more resilient with a decline of only 0.8%.
  • Palantir's stock rallied 15% for the week due to its potential involvement in the Iran conflict, highlighting the complex and unpredictable nature of market reactions to geopolitical events.
  • The Technology sector experienced a decline of 1.6%, while Consumer Services and Energy were among the leaders with declines of only 0.3% and 0.8%, respectively, in a week marked by increased uncertainty and volatility in global markets.

As I reflect on the week ending March 7, 2026, it's clear that markets were heavily influenced by the escalating conflict in Iran. The S&P 500, a broad benchmark of the US stock market, fell by 2.0%, while the Dow Jones declined by 2.9%. The Nasdaq, often seen as a proxy for technology stocks, dropped by 1.4%. This downward trend was echoed across various sectors and indices, with the S&P MidCap 400 and Russell 2000 experiencing more significant declines of 5.4% and 4.9%, respectively.

The news headlines from the past week provide valuable context for understanding these market movements. The ongoing war in Iran has led to increased tensions and supply chain disruptions, particularly in the energy sector. This is reflected in the surge in US pump prices and the boost to US Gulf oil prices, which have reached their highest levels since 2020. As a result, the Energy sector was one of the more resilient performers, with a decline of only 0.8%. In contrast, sectors like Materials and Industrials, which are more closely tied to global trade and supply chains, experienced more significant declines of 6.4% and 5.0%, respectively.

Among the numerous news stories that shaped the week's market action, a few stood out as particularly significant. The escalation of the Iran conflict, with reports of drone attacks, missile strikes, and rising civilian casualties, has led to increased uncertainty and volatility in global markets. The White House's call for bolder action on energy prices and the announcement of plans to quadruple production of certain weaponry also made headlines. Additionally, the performance of companies like Palantir, which rallied 15% for the week due to its potential involvement in the conflict, highlights the complex and often unpredictable nature of market reactions to geopolitical events.

In terms of sector performance, it's worth noting that Consumer Services and Energy were among the leaders, with declines of only 0.3% and 0.8%, respectively. On the other hand, sectors like Healthcare and Materials lagged significantly, with declines of 3.7% and 6.4%, respectively. The Technology sector, which is often seen as a bellwether for the broader market, experienced a decline of 1.6%. Company-specific news, such as Boeing's potential huge deal and Google's announcement regarding Anthropic, also contributed to the market's volatility.

As we look ahead to the coming week, it's clear that the ongoing conflict in Iran will remain a major focus for markets. The potential for further escalation, combined with the complex web of global alliances and interests, makes it difficult to predict exactly how events will unfold. However, by staying informed and up-to-date on the latest developments, we can better understand the market's reactions and navigate the resulting uncertainty.
